# SD_01 車輛租用系統 — Sequence Diagram

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    actor RentalUser
    participant Account
    participant RentalOrder
    participant Vehicle
    participant RentalPeriod
    participant RentalFee

    Note right of RentalUser: 租車用戶
    Note right of Account: 帳戶
    Note right of RentalOrder: 租車訂單
    Note right of Vehicle: 車輛(車型)
    Note right of RentalPeriod: 租用時間區間
    Note right of RentalFee: 租金

    RentalUser->>Account: RegisterAccount(username, password, email)
    Note right of Account: 註冊帳號
    Account-->>RentalUser: registered
    Note right of RentalUser: 註冊完成

    RentalUser->>Account: Login(username, password)
    Note right of Account: 登入
    Account-->>RentalUser: loggedIn
    Note right of RentalUser: 登入成功才可租車

    RentalUser->>RentalOrder: ToRentalCar(vehicleType, startDate, endDate)
    Note right of RentalOrder: 線上租車(包含以下 4 件事情)
    activate RentalOrder

    RentalOrder->>Vehicle: SelectCarType(vehicleType)
    Note right of Vehicle: 選擇車型(Car / SUV / Truck / SportsCar / ElectricCar)
    Vehicle-->>RentalOrder: vehicle

    RentalOrder->>RentalPeriod: SelectPeriod(startDate, endDate)
    Note right of RentalPeriod: 選擇租用時間區間
    RentalPeriod-->>RentalOrder: period

    RentalOrder->>RentalFee: CalculateFee(vehicle, period)
    Note right of RentalFee: 計算租金
    RentalFee-->>RentalOrder: totalFee

    RentalOrder->>RentalOrder: ConfirmRental()
    Note right of RentalOrder: 確認租車

    deactivate RentalOrder
    RentalOrder-->>RentalUser: rentalConfirmed
    Note right of RentalUser: 回傳租車結果
```

## 說明

- 領域物件由左至右依訊息呼叫順序排列：Account → RentalOrder → Vehicle → RentalPeriod → RentalFee。
- 租車前須先呼叫 `Account.RegisterAccount()` 與 `Account.Login()`。
- 「選擇車型、選擇租用時間區間、計算租金、確認租車」4 件事情皆包含在 `ToRentalCar()` 之中。
